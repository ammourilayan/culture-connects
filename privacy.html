Privacy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Culture Connects is a guide to the cultures of the world. It's built to collect as little about you as possible.

Exploring countries

You don't need an account to explore. Your passport stamps and settings, like the daily country reminder, are saved only on your own device. We don't use ads, trackers or analytics, and we don't sell or share information about you.

The Teachers' Lounge

If you join the Teachers' Lounge, we keep your name and email address so you can sign in. Lessons, files, comments and posts you share there can be seen by everyone who visits the site. You can delete anything you've posted at any time.

The Lounge is run on Supabase, which stores this information securely on our behalf.

Notifications

In the app, you can choose to get a "country of the day" reminder. It's scheduled on your phone itself, and you can turn it off any time.

Children

Culture Connects is for everyone to explore. The Teachers' Lounge is for adults, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your information

To delete your Teachers' Lounge account and everything connected to it, email us and we'll remove it.
