Putting Culture Connects on your own website

About an hour from start to finish. Do the parts in order. If anything looks different from what's described here, take a screenshot and send it to Claude.

What's in this folder

  1. index.html: the whole website.
  2. setup.sql: a one-time setup script for the Teachers' Lounge.
  3. SETUP-GUIDE.html: this guide.

Part 1 · Get your .com

About 10 minutes · about $10–15 a year

  1. Go to namecheap.com or porkbun.com and search for cultureconnects.com.
  2. If it's taken, try one of the backups, like thecultureconnects.com or cultureconnectsworld.com.
  3. Buy it. Turn off extras like hosting, email or "site builder." You don't need them.
Keep that tab open. You'll come back to it in Part 4.

Part 2 · Set up the Teachers' Lounge (Supabase)

About 15 minutes · free

  1. Go to supabase.com, tap Start your project, and sign up.
  2. Tap New project. Name it Culture Connects, make up a database password (save it somewhere safe), choose the region West US, and tap Create. Give it about 2 minutes to finish.
  3. In the left menu, open SQL Editor and tap New query.
  4. Open setup.sql, copy everything in it, paste it into the editor, and tap Run. You should see "Success."
  5. Go to Project Settings → API (sometimes called Data API). Copy two things: the Project URL and the anon public key.
  6. Send both to Claude, and Claude will put them into your site for you. (These two are meant to be public, so they're safe to share. Never share the service_role key or your database password.)
  7. Go to Authentication → URL Configuration. Set Site URL to your new address, for example https://cultureconnects.com. Under Redirect URLs, add https://cultureconnects.com/**.
Good to know: Supabase's built-in email only sends a few sign-in emails per hour. That's fine while you test. Before you invite lots of teachers, connect a free email sender (for example Resend) under Authentication → Emails → SMTP Settings, and Claude can walk you through it.

Part 3 · Put the site online (Netlify)

About 10 minutes · free

  1. Go to netlify.com and sign up. Using your Google account is easiest.
  2. Tap Add new site → Deploy manually.
  3. Drag the whole folder (the one with index.html) onto the page. This works best on a computer.
  4. In a few seconds you'll get a link like something-random.netlify.app. Open it. Your site is live!
  5. Optional: in Site configuration → Change site name, rename it to something like cultureconnects.

Part 4 · Connect your .com

About 10 minutes, then up to a day to take effect

  1. In Netlify, open your site and go to Domain management → Add a domain. Type your .com and follow the steps.
  2. Netlify gives you a short list of name servers (they look like dns1.p01.nsone.net).
  3. Go back to where you bought the .com, find Nameservers, choose Custom, paste in Netlify's, and save.
  4. Wait. It usually works within an hour, and sometimes it takes up to a day. Netlify adds the secure padlock (https) automatically.

Part 5 · Make yourself the moderator

About 3 minutes

  1. On your live site, scroll to Teachers' Lounge, enter your name and email, and tap the sign-in link in your email.
  2. Back in Supabase, open SQL Editor → New query and paste this, using your own email:
    insert into public.admins (user_id) select id from auth.users where email = 'you@example.com' on conflict do nothing;
  3. Tap Run. Now you'll see a Delete button on every lesson and post, so you can remove anything that doesn't belong.

Updating the site later

  1. Ask Claude for changes, and you'll get a new index.html.
  2. In Netlify, open your site → Deploys, and drag the folder in again. That's it. Your lessons and posts stay safe in Supabase.
