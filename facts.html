How we check our facts

Culture Connects is a starting point for curiosity and connection, so getting things right matters to us.

Where the information comes from

Every country page is researched from reputable sources, like government and tourism sites, encyclopedias, universities, cultural organizations and language references. Each country lists its main sources at the bottom of its page.

How it's checked

  • Every entry is checked a second time against sources, separately from when it was written.
  • Details that can't be confirmed are left out or described more generally.
  • Things that change, like holidays, money and current events, are checked again over time.
  • We use careful, kind language: patterns, not rules, and never stereotypes.

What it can't do

No page can describe a whole people. Every country holds many regions, faiths, generations and families. The best guide is always the person in front of you, so ask, listen and let people tell you who they are.

Help us get it right

If you're from a place on this site, or you notice something that's wrong or missing, please tell us. Every country page has a Suggest a correction link, or you can email hello@explorecultureconnects.com. We read every message.
